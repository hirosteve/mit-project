Design decisions

Protected attributes excluded. Gender and marital status were removed from the feature space and retained only for fairness auditing. The audit tested disparate impact, recall parity, and whether either attribute could be reconstructed from the remaining features.

Conduct variable excluded. Whether penalty compensation had been charged was withheld, because compensation is levied as a consequence of missed repayment rather than being available when a facility is assessed. Including it raised measured performance but would not be available in use.

What the model does not use. No weather, rainfall, crop yield or agronomic data. Rural context enters only through the client settlement classification. Claims about environmental risk are outside what this model measures.

Trained and evaluated in the accompanying analysis notebook. Preprocessing, resampling and scaling were fitted inside cross-validation folds so that no validation data influenced model fitting.
